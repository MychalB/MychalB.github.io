What I’ve Learned About Prompting After 5 Weeks of Testing

After spending five weeks working with AI image tools like Midjourney, Firefly, and ChatGPT’s image generator, I’ve realized something important: prompting isn’t about being fancy — it’s about being intentional.

Early on, I thought better prompts meant longer prompts. But what actually improved my results was clarity. When I clearly defined lighting, mood, composition, and subject, the output improved dramatically.

Here are three things that consistently made my results stronger:

  • Specifying lighting (soft daylight, cinematic shadows, studio lighting)
  • Describing emotional tone (calm, chaotic, introspective, surreal)
  • Adding composition direction (close-up portrait, wide-angle shot, centered subject)

The biggest shift wasn’t technical — it was mental. I stopped asking, “What cool image can I get?” and started asking, “What am I trying to communicate?”

That mindset change made AI feel less like a trick and more like a creative tool.
